OUTSIDE THE GUARANTEE

Explicit boundaries.

01Observed HTTP only. No proof for unrecorded endpoints, WebSockets, or SSE.
02Redaction is a safeguard, not a privacy guarantee. Review every cassette.
03Verification can trigger side effects. Use disposable test tenants, not production.
